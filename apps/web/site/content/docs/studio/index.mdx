Every workflow has a **Start** node, and as many [triggers](/docs/studio/triggers) as it needs.
Each action you add is a node: its icon, its name and component, its inputs on the left and its
outputs on the right. Select one to edit it in the **inspector** — its settings, the data it can
bind to (**Input**) and what it publishes (**Output**). A node says what's off about it: a required
setting left empty (**!**), or something the board doesn't have (**⊘** — an HTTP node on a board
without Wi-Fi).

## Outputs decide what runs next

When an action finishes, it ends on one of its outputs:

- **Success** — it did what it does.
- **Fallback** (red, at the bottom) — it couldn't: a missing value, a pin that failed, a variable
  store that's full…
- **Custom outputs** — some actions branch further (a comparison's *true* / *false*, for example).

The wire leaving that output is the next action. An output with nothing connected ends the pass:
in **loop** mode (Start's *Mode*) the flow returns to Start, in **once** mode it stops.

## Working on the canvas

- The **+** in the canvas' corner adds an action or a trigger — components listed alphabetically,
  triggers first with their green mark. Hover a wire and press **+** to insert one in the middle of
  it; drop a wire on empty canvas to add one there.
- Each setting's **ⓘ** shows what it's for. **Notes** are only for you — the board never sees them.
- The **eye** on a node disables it: the flow skips it and goes straight on to where its Success
  output leads, without unwiring anything. Configs bound to its outputs read nothing while it's off.
  Press it again to bring it back.
- The minimap (top right) can be dragged and zoomed to move around large workflows.
- The workflow's **Settings** (⚙ next to its name) changes its **board** and its platform version
  and limits.
