A monochrome I2C OLED — SSD1306 (0.96", 128x64 or 128x32) or SH1106 (1.3", 128x64), 4 pins. Print text in 3 fonts and 4 sizes, Scroll it, Draw shapes (any shape from its vertices), show an Image (built-in icons or your own bitmap), and readings as a Bar, a Gauge or a Chart over time. Actions draw into a buffer and Update Screen sends it — turn it off on all but the last to build a screen without flicker.

## Actions

### Print

Writes text — or a bound value, like a sensor's reading — on a line of the screen, in one of 4 sizes.

**Settings**

| Setting | Type | Default | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SDA Pin** | Pin (digital out) | GPIO21 | **Required.** Board pin wired to the display's SDA. |
| **SCL Pin** | Pin (digital out) | GPIO22 | **Required.** Board pin wired to the display's SCL (sometimes labeled SCK). |
| **Model** | One of: SSD1306 · 128x64 (0.96"), SSD1306 · 128x32 (0.91"), SH1106 · 128x64 (1.3") | SSD1306 · 128x64 (0.96") | The display's controller and size — 0.96" modules are usually SSD1306, 1.3" ones SH1106 (picking SSD1306 for one shows a shifted, noisy image). |
| **Text** | Text | `Hello!` | What to show — type it, or bind an output or a variable (numbers are formatted, see Decimals). A typed \n goes on to the next line. Accents show as their plain letter. |
| **Line** | Number (1 to 8) | `1` | Which line to write on, from 1 at the top — lines are 8 px: 1 to 8 on a 64-px screen, 1 to 4 on a 32-px one. A bigger Text Size takes more lines from there down. |
| **Font** | One of: Standard · 8x8, Small · 5x7, Bold · 8x8 | Standard · 8x8 | The typeface — Standard (8 px a character, 16 a line), Small (a finer 5x7, 6 px a character: 21 a line, with descenders) or Bold (Standard 1 px thicker, 9 px a character: 14 a line). |
| **Text Size** | One of: 1x · 8 px, 2x · 16 px, 3x · 24 px, 4x · 32 px | 1x · 8 px | How big the text is — 1x to 4x the font (8, 16, 24 or 32 px tall), taking that many 8-px lines from Line down. |
| **Align** | One of: Left, Center, Right | Left | Where on the line the text goes. |
| **Column** | Number (1 to 21) | `1` | *Advanced.* *Shown when Align is Left.* Which character the text starts at, from 1 — up to 16 in Standard at 1x, 21 in Small (fewer at bigger sizes). |
| **Format** | Text | — | *Advanced.* Text around Text — \{\} stands for it: "Temp: \{\} C". Without \{\}, it goes before it. |
| **Decimals** | Number (0 to 6) | `2` | *Advanced.* Decimal places of a bound number that isn't whole, from 0 to 6. |
| **Clear Line** | On / off | Blank first | *Advanced.* Blank the line (all of its height) before writing, so a shorter text doesn't leave the end of the last one behind. |
| **Update Screen** | On / off | Show now | *Advanced.* Send the drawing to the screen now. Turn it off to keep drawing with the next actions and show it all at once with the last one (no flicker). |
| **Address** | Text | `0x3C` | *Advanced.* The display's I2C address, in hex — 0x3C on most modules, 0x3D on some (set by a jumper on the back). |
