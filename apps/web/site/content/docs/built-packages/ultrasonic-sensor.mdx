Measures distance with an HC-SR04-style ultrasonic sensor (also JSN-SR04T and other 4-pin modules) — Measure publishes how far the nearest object is, Detect branches on whether something is closer than a threshold. The HC-SR04's Echo is 5 V: on a 3.3 V board (ESP32) wire it through a voltage divider (e.g. 1 kΩ / 2 kΩ), or use a 3.3 V module.

| Node | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Measure](#measure) | Action | Pings and publishes the distance to the nearest object. |
| [Detect](#detect) | Action | Pings and goes out Near when something is closer than Threshold, Far otherwise (nothing in range counts as Far). |

## Actions

### Measure

Pings and publishes the distance to the nearest object. No echo (nothing in range) goes out Fallback.

**Settings**

| Setting | Type | Default | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trigger Pin** | Pin (digital out) | — | Board pin wired to the sensor's Trig. |
| **Echo Pin** | Pin (digital in) | — | Board pin wired to the sensor's Echo (through a divider on a 3.3 V board). |
| **Unit** | One of: Centimeters, Millimeters, Inches | Centimeters | Unit of the distance it publishes. |
| **Timeout** | Number | `30` | *Advanced.* How long to wait for the echo, in milliseconds. 30 covers ~5 m; lower it to give up sooner on far objects. |
| **Temperature** | Number | `20` | *Advanced.* Air temperature, in °C — sound travels faster in warm air. 20 is fine indoors. |

**Publishes**

| Output | Type | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `distance` | float | Distance to the nearest object, in Unit. |
| `echo` | int | How long the echo took to come back, in microseconds. |

### Detect

Pings and goes out Near when something is closer than Threshold, Far otherwise (nothing in range counts as Far).

Ends on **Near**, **Far** — or **Fallback** when it can't run.

**Settings**

| Setting | Type | Default | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trigger Pin** | Pin (digital out) | — | Board pin wired to the sensor's Trig. |
| **Echo Pin** | Pin (digital in) | — | Board pin wired to the sensor's Echo (through a divider on a 3.3 V board). |
| **Threshold** | Number | `20` | Closer than this (in Unit) goes out Near. |
| **Unit** | One of: Centimeters, Millimeters, Inches | Centimeters | Unit of Threshold and of the distance it publishes. |
| **Timeout** | Number | `30` | *Advanced.* How long to wait for the echo, in milliseconds. 30 covers ~5 m. |
| **Temperature** | Number | `20` | *Advanced.* Air temperature, in °C — sound travels faster in warm air. 20 is fine indoors. |

**Publishes**

| Output | Type | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `distance` | float | Distance to the nearest object, in Unit — only when an echo came back. |
| `near` | bool | Whether something was closer than Threshold. |
